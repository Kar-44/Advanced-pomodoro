The Pomodoro Technique enhances focus by breaking work into timed intervals separated by short breaks, reducing mental fatigue and sustaining deep concentration. The Recall phase leverages active retrieval — one of cognitive science's most powerful memory consolidation strategies — by prompting you to review what you just studied before moving on. Completing multiple cycles before a Long Break allows your brain to fully consolidate learning while restoring sustained mental energy.
